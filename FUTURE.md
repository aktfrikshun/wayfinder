# Future Enhancements

## 1) Spiritual Growth Tracking

Add a "Spiritual" component to each child's long-term growth profile so families can capture and reflect on faith-based development alongside educational, emotional, and mental growth.

### Example Data To Capture
- Churches, temples, mosques, or other faith communities attended
- Youth group participation and spiritual education programs
- Community service and charitable functions
- Volunteer activities and mission/service events
- Personal milestones (for example: baptisms, confirmations, dedications)
- Reflections from parents/caregivers on values, character, and purpose development

### Potential Product Additions
- New `Spiritual Events` timeline entries linked to a child
- Optional tags for faith community, event type, and values/themes
- Attachments for certificates, photos, handouts, or notes
- Narrative insights that connect spiritual participation with broader child development patterns
- Privacy controls so families can decide who can view spiritual records
